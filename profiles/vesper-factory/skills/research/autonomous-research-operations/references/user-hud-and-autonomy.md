# User-facing Research Operations Preferences

- User wants set-and-forget execution of **approved** research, not repeated “shall I continue?” pauses.
- Keep the user watching a plain, stable terminal HUD: current phase, GPU use, small NN architecture line, evidence bars, queue and next work. Avoid flashy panels, blinking redraws, and fake progress.
- The user values ambitious research and will push back on premature stopping. Phrase conclusions narrowly: close the tested claim, state the evidence, and identify the next branch that changes information or structure.
- For financial research, separate descriptive representation findings from alpha, calibrated forecasting, and deployability. Preserve point-in-time provenance and simple-baseline comparisons.
- Sustained GPU use is welcome for fixed replication/ablation batches; do not confuse utilization with a reason to perform unbounded hypothesis fishing.
